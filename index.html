THE METHOD
Read shapes, not values.
Payload Radar compares the structure observed across your samples: field paths, types, presence, and array items. It helps you spot changes that deserve a closer look before a release.
01
Bring more than one sample
Auto detect accepts a complete JSON document or JSON Lines independently on each side. You can override either format. A single JSON document may be an array; a top-level array remains one document, and its items are analyzed together.
02
Evidence has limits
An observed field is not a guaranteed contract. Missing fields, small samples, and empty arrays can limit what we know. Review the evidence alongside your API specification.
03
Protect what your consumer needs
Consumer Lens checks the allowed types and required status you explicitly select for each field. It does not inspect your app’s code. A container rule does not automatically check its children. Export a lens to run the same checks in CI without raw sample values.
04
Your data stays yours
Analysis happens in this browser. No payloads are sent to a server. Reports and exported lenses contain field paths, types and counts, but no raw sample values. Review field names before sharing.
